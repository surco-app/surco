# traktor-shared-cover-ids.nml

A history `.nml` written by Traktor (`NML VERSION="19"`), copied unchanged from
`tests/fixtures/history.nml` of https://github.com/wolkenarchitekt/traktor-nml-utils at
commit `ccee77786e21e0b41674fd3d3b9553a95f9d3e5f` (GPL-3.0, the same licence as Surco).

It is the real collection the tests have where Traktor gives one `COVERARTID` to several
tracks. Four tracks of *Supdub Resume vol. 1* share one id, and two tracks from different
releases (Inphasia & Nodin, Boris Brejcha) share another because they carry the same image.
